# Exact finite identification examples

Registered 2026-09-28 before execution. Explanatory construction, not a new theorem or a statistical study. No training or hypothesis significance claim.

Y is uniform on {0,1,2}, B = 1[Y=0]. Compare C_valid=B, C_redundant=1[Y=0] computed from the label, and C_complement=1[Y=1] computed from the label. Enumerate their channel histograms, standalone and joint Bayes accuracy, conditional mutual information and label-only interventions with B clamped. Expected result: equal binary cardinality, histogram and standalone accuracy, but joint accuracy differs; target dependence can coexist with zero conditional information. If enumeration differs, retain outcome and repair the argument. Enumerate all y substitutions rather than estimate. Budget: local CPU <1 minute, $0.
